Operating intent

Built around the home setup, GBRS-style training, the daily mobility programming, a WHOOP goal of more Zone 4/5 exposure, and the need to stay durable rather than cooked. Training identity: strong, mobile, athletic, durable, hard to break.

Mobility opens the pattern. Main lifts drive strength. Accessory pairings create density. Burnouts add the edge. Recovery protects the adaptation.

Canon

Ranked sources: GBRS / Vernon Griffith first, then Elastaboy, Functional Bodybuilding, Functional Patterns last and narrow.

Main lift protected
Squat, bench (floor press if no bench), deadlift. Straight sets, full rest, never supersetted. Dips are an accessory.
Athletic dose
The hardest day includes a small jump / land / elastic slot right after the deadlift. No extra hinge or lunge volume on deadlift day.
Mobility ladder
Same drills, Level 1 / 2 / 3. Earn the next level when control is clean. Recovery days stay unloaded.
Burnout complementarity
Do not repeat the day’s main lift or its heaviest accessories. Condition with the leftover patterns.
Recovery stays clean
No sneaky lift on Day 3 or 6. Day 5 stays submaximal.

Daily session template

Targeted myofascial release
3–5 minutes. Only hit areas that improve today's mobility or lifting. 10–15 minutes on recovery days.
Mobility programming
6–15 minutes. Same drills, Level 1 / 2 / 3. Earn the next level when control is clean. Recovery days stay unloaded.
Main lift
15–25 minutes. Straight sets, full rest.
Accessory pairings
15–25 minutes. Efficient, non-competing supersets.
Burnout
10 minutes. Zone 4/5 exposure on hard days. Three hard burnouts plus one optional moderate each week.
Downshift
2–5 minutes. Breathing, easy walk, light rolling.

Typical total: 60–75 minutes.

Programming rules

Never superset the main lift
Squat, bench, and deadlift get full rest and clean RPE readings. Floor press if no bench.
Pair accessories intelligently
Pair non-competing movements after the main lift.
Avoid spine-loaded pairings
No RDL + row, deadlift + heavy carry, or heavy hinge + heavy trunk pairing.
Avoid grip bottlenecks
Do not stack pull-ups, RDLs, hanging core, and carries so the forearms fail first.
Burnouts support the block
They build work capacity; they do not steal from strength progression.
Athletic dose
Hardest day gets a small jump / land / elastic slot right after the deadlift. Mobility already covers the split lunge — do not pile lunges on deadlift day.
Burnout complementarity
Do not repeat the day’s main lift pattern or its heaviest accessories.
Day 5 stays submaximal
Durability / chassis day, not a fourth hard strength day.
Recovery days stay clean
No sneaky “quick lift” on Day 3 or Day 6.

Main lift rule

Add load only when the reps are clean. No grinders. No ego reps. No conditioning disguised as strength. Strength should progress, but the program should never become a daily test of willpower.

Myofascial rule

Release only what improves the next movement. Do not roll for 20 minutes out of obligation, and do not hit every area every day — take the areas that match the session.

Burnout rules

Duration
10 minutes.
Goal
Zone 4 by minute 4–6; occasional Zone 5 near the end.
Frequency
Three hard burnouts plus one optional moderate.
Technique
Never chase WHOOP at the expense of form.
Scaling
Reduce reps or load before stopping.
Week 5
One chaotic expression: Day 4 benchmark at 85–90%. Other burnouts that week are 85–90% and not benchmarks. Deadlift stays a clean 5 × 3.
Week 6
No redline conditioning.
Red recovery day
Skip the burnout or perform a 60–70% easy version.

Burnout effort

Day 1
Hinge / Push / Carry Engine · 85–90%.
Day 2
Swing / Crawl / Sprawl · 85–90%.
Day 4
Light Squat + Burpee Couplet · 90–95% in Weeks 1–4; 85–90% benchmark in Week 5.
Day 5
Carry / Crawl / Swing · optional, 70–85%.

Burnout scaling

Grip fails before breathing
Reduce pull-ups and carries.
Legs fail before heart rate climbs
Reduce squat and lunge volume.
Push-ups become singles
Elevate hands or reduce reps.
Barbell technique degrades
Drop load immediately.
Heart rate never climbs
Reduce transitions, pick faster movements.
Heart rate spikes but movement gets sloppy
Slow slightly and keep moving.

Readiness rules

Green
Full session + full burnout. Add load or reps if clean, not chaos.
Average
Full strength, moderate burnout.
Red
Strength only or recovery day. Burnout off. Heavy hinge off.
Achilles cranky
Keep strength, reduce jumps and burpees, emphasize carries and swings.
Back feels cooked
Avoid heavy hinge; swap deadlift for RDL or goblet work.
Shoulder feels pinchy
Skip dips, use push-ups or DB press, increase scap work.
Motivation low but body okay
Start the session; cut the burnout if needed.

Redundancy guardrails

The mobility program already carries real strengthening volume. Extra calf work stays on Day 5. No extra tib block unless the Achilles is cranky. Petersons stay but lower. Keep the deadlift clean and reduce hinge accessories. Carries cover the glute work — no extra fluff. Never lift heavy upper body on Day 3.

Week at a glance

Day 1
Ankles & Knees. Squat. Pull-ups + Petersons; RDL separate, straps if needed. Hinge / push / carry engine.
Day 2
Hips & Glutes. Bench (floor press if no bench). Row + DB OHP; dips/push-ups + farmer carry. Swing / crawl / sprawl.
Day 3
Spine & Shoulders. No lift. Zone 2 + tissue + breath. Mobility ladder without Level 3 load. No burnout.
Day 4
Ankles & posterior chain. Deadlift, then elastic / landing. Push press + chin-ups; suitcase. Light squat + burpees.
Day 5
Full-body light reset. No heavy main lift. Controlled chassis circuit. Optional carry / crawl / swing.
Day 6
Follow-me flow, 3 rounds × 60 sec. Walk / tissue / breath. No burnout.
Day 7
Optional easy mobility. Rest. No lift.

Progression

Protect the main lift. Pair the accessories. Condition with intent. Recover like adaptation depends on it.
