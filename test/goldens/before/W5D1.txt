== TISSUE ==
dose: 3–5 min
goal: Prep squatting, knee travel, ankle range.
1. Calves · Roller · 60 sec / side
   cue: Roll from the Achilles up toward the back of the knee. Pause on dense spots.
2. Front thigh / hip-flexor quad · Roller or ball · 45–60 sec / side
   cue: Work the upper front thigh, especially where the quad meets the hip.
3. Outer thigh / outer quad · Roller · 30–45 sec / side
   cue: Moderate pressure along the outside of the thigh. Don't smash the side of the knee.
4. Glutes · Hard medicine ball · 60 sec / side
   cue: Sit on the ball and search the big glute and side-glute pocket.
5. Inner thighs · Roller · 45 sec / side
   cue: Gentle pressure before squats and Petersons.
note: Priority order for squat day: calves and front thigh first, then outer quad and glute. Skip the last item if time is short. Don't smash the IT band — work outer quad, hip, and glute instead.

== PREP PUMP ==
dose: 4–6 min
goal: Prime squat and knee-over-toe without stealing the main.
gatedOnAmberRedOrFlag: yes
1. Goblet squat pulse · Light KB · 2 × 8
   cue: Heels down, knees track, no bounce.
2. Split-stance lean · 6 / side
   cue: Front knee over the toe, heel stays.
note: Warm the pattern. This is not a working set.

== MOBILITY ==
mobNote: Elastaboy ladder. Start at Level 1. Earn Level 2, then Level 3 only when today's level is clean. Do not skip.
weekMob: Stay on a level you own. No new level.
- Knee-to-toe ankle rockers — Dorsiflexion, knee-over-toe comfort.
  levels: Hands-guided, heel down. | More knee travel. | Light load on the knee.
- Straight-leg calf raisers — Gastroc and Achilles.
  levels: Two-leg, slight lean. | More lean. | Single-leg.
- Bent-knee calf raisers — Soleus capacity.
  levels: Two-leg, shallow bend. | Deeper bend. | Single-leg on a ledge.
- Tib raisers — Ankle balance, front-of-shin strength.
  levels: Back to wall, feet close. | Feet farther from the wall. | Slant-board.
- Slant-board squats — Knee confidence, squat tolerance.
  levels: Bodyweight, no bounce. | Light load. | Hindu / self-elevated heels only if Level 2 is owned.
