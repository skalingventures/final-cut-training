mobNote: Elastaboy ladder. Chassis day. Start at Level 1; Level 3 only if you already own it.
weekMob: Earn the next level only when today's level is clean. Do not skip.

  levels: Fingertips toward the knees. | Wide stance, hands on head. | Feet together, dumbbell in one hand, no twist.
- Cobra press-ups — Spinal extension and chest opening.
  levels: Forearm sphinx. | Full hand lockout. | Shins to the wall.
- Prone scap W + reach — Upper-back activation.
  levels: On a bench. | On the floor. | Light dumbbells or plates.
- Hammer grip wrist rockers — Wrist prep.
  levels: Seated, gentle. | More range and pressure.
- Laying neck controlled rotations — Neck mobility.
  levels: Head lift, look side to side. | Slightly more range, or a light plate on the forehead.
- Laying neck double chins — Deep neck flexors.
  levels: Floor chin tuck. | Head off a bench plus a light plate.
